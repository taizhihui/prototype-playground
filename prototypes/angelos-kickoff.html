What if every product question
became a sale?

You've built Angelos on customer education. A first-time buyer who's never painted in their life gets a starter kit — but if they don't know where to start, or the paint doesn't work for their project, they don't come back. And often, they don't tell you why.

AI Journey closes that gap. A shopper abandons a cart wondering if the paint works for their specific project — the AI checks in and answers. A first-time buyer gets their order — the AI follows up with step-one guidance and an open door. Here's what both flows look like for Angelos.

Flow 1 — Cart Recovery
Goal Convert first-time buyers who abandoned a cart to a second purchase within 90 days.
Audience First-time Angelos Direct buyers who purchased in the last 60 days and abandoned a cart without completing.
Guardrails Max 2 messages/week  ·  Message 1 is a reminder (no discount)  ·  Message 2 adds 15% if no conversion  ·  No messages 8pm–9am  ·  Suppress if active conversation or open support ticket
Flow Intelligence
Behavioral inputs that drive this flow's decisions
Use-Case Questions
Question first
Cart abandons are rarely about price
For paint and hobby brands, the most common cart abandon is a use-case question: will this work for my specific project? The flow leads with the product promise — answering before the shopper has to ask, and never opening with a discount.
Purchase Context
Always on
What they own informs what they need next
AI Journey carries what they already bought. When Alex reaches back out about individual paint colors, the AI already knows he started with the Bestsellers Kit — so it can surface the right complementary colors and accessories without starting from scratch.
Compliance Guardrails
Built in
Opt-out protection, not an afterthought
Quiet hours enforced automatically. 7-day same-type cooldown. Opt-out processed instantly — no gray area. The opt-out rate is front-page data for you every week, not a metric that hides in a report.
Timing Window
1.5–3h
Optimal outreach after cart abandon
Shopper is still warm, not asleep. Container holds overnight sends until 9am local. If the abandon happens after 7pm, message goes out at 9am the next morning — not at 2am.
Flow 2 — Post-Purchase Education
Goal Build product confidence in first-time buyers to drive the first repeat purchase within 30–90 days.
Audience First-time Angelos Direct buyers within 24 hours of their first completed order.
Guardrails 1 message per purchase  ·  No discounts — education only  ·  Always end with open-door CTA  ·  Escalate to human team if question exceeds AI knowledge
Flow Intelligence
The signals and structure behind the post-purchase education flow
Education as Retention
1–3 mo
Natural repeat purchase cycle
If they feel like their hands are held and the product works as expected, they'll spend $60 instead of $30 on the next order. This flow exists for that transition — not to push the next sale, but to make the first one work.
Product-Context Aware
Item-level
Tips match what they actually bought
A Starter Kit gets starter tips. A Bestsellers Kit gets different guidance. The AI knows what's in the order and sends advice specific to it — not a generic "thanks for your purchase" message with a link to the help center.
Open-Door Positioning
Always on
"We're here if you need anything"
Every post-purchase message ends with an open invitation to ask questions. This is the brand Tyler described: family-owned since 1907, accessible, expert. The CTA isn't a link — it's a standing offer to help with the project.
Human Escalation
Seamless
Complex questions hand off to the team
If a shopper asks something the AI can't confidently answer — unusual substrates, specific product interactions, edge cases — it escalates to the Angelos team without friction. The shopper never hits a dead end.

60-Day Kickoff

Three things to measure. Two flows to start.

This is the evaluation framework for the trial. Not vanity metrics — the three signals that tell you whether the model is working.

Guardrail
≤2%
Opt-out rate
Why this matters for you
You paused SMS over the Texas litigation concerns — that's the right instinct. Opt-out rate is the single best proxy for whether messages are relevant and well-timed. If shoppers are opting out, the AI is messaging too often, too broadly, or with the wrong content. We treat this as a hard guardrail: if it crosses 1.5%, we review the flow before the next send.
Proof of concept
≥15%
Reply rate
Why this matters for you
Your whole model is built on being accessible — "we're here if you need anything" is part of the product experience. Reply rate is the proof that shoppers are actually using that door. When a first-time buyer responds to ask about their project, it signals the hand-holding is working and the relationship is forming. A 15%+ reply rate (Klaviyo one-way SMS averages 1–3% click-through) means the conversational model is earning its keep.
North star
90-day
Repeat purchase rate
Why this matters for you
You said it clearly: a bad product experience is a permanent customer loss. Repeat purchase rate is the best signal that the conversation is doing its job — helping shoppers choose the right product, feel confident, and come back. We set a baseline in week 1, then compare the AI Journey cohort against it at 60 days.
Flow Selection

Which flow first?

Two flows · Both agreed in kickoff
Week 1–2
Cart Abandonment
Reminder first · 15% discount on message 2 if no conversion
Cart abandon is the clearest intent signal — the shopper already chose a product. Message 1 is a reminder with no discount, leading with the product promise and an open question. If no conversion, message 2 adds 15%. Tyler's existing Klaviyo cart flow uses 15% — matching that pattern on SMS ensures consistency while we establish the new channel.
Week 3–4
Post-Purchase Education
Triggered 24h after first order · No discount · Education only
Fires once per first-time purchase. Sends product-specific usage tips, ends with an open invitation to ask questions. This is the "family-owned since 1907, we know this stuff" moment — building the relationship that drives the 1–3 month reorder. Calibrated after cart recovery is live so we can test the voice on a simpler flow first.
