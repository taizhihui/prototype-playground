Container Concept · ONE BONE

From trigger logic
to goal-driven engagement

Today, AI Journey runs flows and campaigns as separate pre-configured sequences. A marketer sets a trigger. A sequence fires. A message goes out.

The container model inverts this. A marketer describes the outcome they want. AI Journey evaluates every shopper, every incoming signal, and decides in real time — who to engage, when, and with what message.

Here's what that looks like for ONE BONE.

ONE BONE first-time buyers most likely to reorder between days 30–60. Container weights all signals higher during this window — cart, browse, campaign, and SA sessions.
Purchase Progression
Tees → Bottoms
Most common 1st→2nd order path
First-time tee buyers most likely to return for Canvas Pants or Athletic Shorts. Container surfaces the right catalog match based on what they already own.
Suppression Logic
4 conditions
When the container holds
Active conversation open · Support ticket unresolved · Message sent within 7 days · Shopper replied "not now" within 48h. Container holds and re-evaluates on the next signal — does not skip.
Signal Hierarchy
SA › Cart › Browse
Intent ranking
Container leads with the highest-intent signal when multiple are active. Shopping Assistant session outranks cart abandon — purchase blocker is already resolved, context is already in hand.
The Shift

Today vs. container

Today
Cart abandon flow fires when a cart is abandoned — regardless of what else is happening with the shopper.
Campaign blasts everyone in the segment at the scheduled send time.
Flows and campaigns run independently — no logic for when they conflict on the same shopper.
Marketer configures a trigger and sequence for each flow type.
Container
Container evaluates all active signals for each shopper and decides which to pursue — or whether to wait.
Campaign becomes one available opportunity, weighed against behavioral signals per shopper.
Container resolves conflicts — merge, prioritize, or suppress — based on each shopper's full context.
Marketer states a goal once. AI handles execution per shopper, per signal.
